Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2025

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

We use Firebase Authentication to allow users to sign in anonymously or via Google Sign-In. When you sign in with Google, we may collect your name, email address, and profile picture. Anonymous sign-ins do not collect personal information.

b. Analytics Data

We collect limited analytics data using Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used. This includes taps, navigation actions, and feature usage events. Data such as device type, OS version, and language may also be collected.

c. Local Storage

Some data is stored locally on your device (e.g., preferences, app state) and is never sent to our servers.

d. Payments and Subscriptions

We use RevenueCat for handling in-app purchases. Payment information is managed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not store any payment data ourselves.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve the App’s functionality
  • Understand user interaction and app performance
  • Manage subscriptions and purchases
  • Ensure proper and secure operation of the App

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

3. Data Sharing

We may share data only with:

  • Firebase (authentication, analytics)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Google Play or Apple App Store (billing and distribution)

All third parties comply with GDPR, COPPA, and other major privacy regulations.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.

5. Your Rights

You may have the right to:

  • Access or delete your data
  • Withdraw consent for analytics
  • Contact us regarding your privacy rights

Contact us at limatevosyann@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our App can be used by children under 13 years of age with parental consent or supervision. We take children’s privacy seriously and comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

We do not knowingly collect more personal information than is reasonably necessary to provide the App’s features. Parents or guardians can review, delete, or manage their child’s information by contacting us at limatevosyann@gmail.com.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete that information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The latest version will always be available in the App and on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at limatevosyann@gmail.com.
